Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Privacy at a Glance

  • No Selling Data: We will never sell, rent, or trade your family's personal information to marketers or advertisers.
  • Strictly Educational: Student data is used exclusively to track learning progress and deliver our curriculum.
  • Parental Control: Parents have full rights to access, review, or request the deletion of their child's data at any time.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Space Code Pro. We understand that enrolling your child in a technology program requires a high level of trust. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, protect, and use the personal information of our students and their parents or legal guardians.

By visiting our facility in Ahmedabad, accessing our website, or enrolling in our courses, you consent to the practices described in this policy. Our procedures are designed to align with strict modern data protection standards, ensuring your family's digital safety.

2. The Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization—meaning we only collect what is absolutely necessary to provide a world-class educational experience. We categorize this data into two distinct groups:

A. Parent and Guardian Data

To manage enrollment, billing, and communication, we collect from the parent/guardian:

  • Contact Details: Full name, email address, primary phone number, and residential address (for billing).
  • Financial Information: Payment details (processed securely via encrypted, certified third-party payment gateways; we do not store full credit card numbers on our servers).
  • Consent Records: Digital signatures or approvals regarding enrollment and media waivers.

B. Student Data

To personalize the learning experience and track academic growth, we collect from the student:

  • Identity Data: First name, last name, age, and current school standard (Std 5-12).
  • Academic Progress: Code written during class, project files, assessment scores, and attendance records.
  • Media (Optional): With explicit parental consent, we may capture photographs or videos of students presenting their projects for our portfolio or social media.

3. How We Use the Information

The information we collect is strictly utilized to operate Space Code Pro effectively and safely. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Deliver Curriculum: Provide access to coding environments, track progress, and tailor instruction to the student's pace.
  • Communicate: Send parents important updates, progress reports, schedule changes, and emergency notifications.
  • Improve Services: Analyze aggregated, non-personally identifiable data to improve our course materials and website functionality.
  • Process Transactions: Send invoices, receipts, and manage enrollment statuses.

4. Children's Privacy (Special Notice)

Protecting the privacy of young children is our highest priority. Because our programs are designed for students between Standards 5 and 12, we operate under strict parental consent guidelines:

  • Direct Collection: We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 18 online without the verifiable consent of a parent or guardian.
  • Educational Use Only: Any data generated by the student (such as code or web projects) is kept within our secure lab environment and used only for educational assessment.
  • If we discover that a minor has provided us with personal information without parental consent, we will delete that information immediately from our servers.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your data. We will only share your information in the following strictly controlled circumstances:

  • Trusted Service Providers: We may share data with vetted third-party vendors who perform services on our behalf (e.g., secure cloud hosting providers, email delivery systems, and payment processors). These vendors are legally bound to strict confidentiality agreements and cannot use your data for their own purposes.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required to do so by law, court order, or to protect the safety, rights, or property of Space Code Pro, our students, or the public.

6. Data Security and Your Rights

Security Measures: We implement rigorous technical and physical security measures, including SSL encryption on our website, secure physical access controls at our Ahmedabad lab, and encrypted databases to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or data breaches.

Your Privacy Rights

As a parent or guardian, you retain full control over your family's data. You have the right to:

  • Access & Review: Request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and your child.
  • Correction: Request corrections to any inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request the complete deletion of your child's data and projects from our systems upon withdrawal from the program (subject to legal billing retention requirements).

To exercise any of these rights, please email us directly.
