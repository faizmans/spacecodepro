Our Promise & Policies

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to Space Code Pro! We believe in clear, fair, and family-friendly guidelines. By enrolling your child in our AI and tech programs, we form a partnership to help them succeed. Here is how we work together.

1. Our Learning Paths

We offer two premium educational formats, both designed to give your child an incredible advantage:

  • The 3-Month Masterclasses: (AI Explorer & AI Scholar). These are deep, foundational journeys where your child builds real skills over time with 3 sessions per week.
  • The 1-Month Sprints: Fast-paced, focused modules designed to teach a specific tech skill rapidly.

To ensure every child gets the attention they deserve, we strictly limit our batches to 10 students. Seats are secured once enrollment is completed.

2. Our 3-Day Happiness Guarantee (Refunds)

We want you and your child to absolutely love learning with us. We have designed our refund policy to be as fair and transparent as possible:

  • 3-Month Masterclasses: We offer a strict 3-day review period. If you feel the program isn't the right fit within the first 3 days of classes starting, simply let us know, and we will issue a pro-rated refund (minus a small administrative fee). After 3 days, no refunds can be processed.
  • 1-Month Sprints: Because these courses are extremely short and accelerated, fees are non-refundable once the very first session begins.

3. The Digital Playground Rules

We teach students how to use Artificial Intelligence as a tool for creativity and learning, not a shortcut. We expect our students to be kind, curious, and respectful.

If a student attempts to use our tools to bypass school homework rules (cheating), generate inappropriate content, or bully other students, we reserve the right to pause or cancel their enrollment without a refund to keep the environment safe for everyone else.

4. Your Child's Creations Belong to Them

Every website, piece of code, or AI-bot your child builds during the course belongs 100% to them. We love showing off how smart our students are! Occasionally, we may ask for your permission to share a screenshot of their amazing project on our website to inspire other parents.

5. We Are Here to Help

Technology changes fast, and sometimes things break (like when ChatGPT goes down globally). While we aren't responsible for global internet or AI platform outages, we promise to always adapt our lessons so your child keeps learning smoothly.
