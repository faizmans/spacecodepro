Privacy & Safety Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At Space Code Pro, your child's digital safety is our number one priority. We treat your family's personal information with the highest level of respect and security. Here is exactly how we protect you.

1. The Information We Hold Gently

To give your child the best learning experience, we only ask for the details we absolutely need:

  • From Parents: Your name, WhatsApp number, and email address so we can send you progress reports and schedules.
  • From Students: Just their first name and grade level to place them in the correct learning batch.

We do not track your child's web browsing outside of our classes, and we do not ask for sensitive personal data.

2. How We Use Your Details

Your data is used for education, never for exploitation. We will never sell, rent, or trade your contact information to third-party marketers or ad agencies.

We only use your email and WhatsApp to send you class links, important updates, and celebrate your child's milestones.

3. The AI Safety Promise (Crucial)

Because we teach students how to master Artificial Intelligence, we have strict rules to protect them from public AI models (like ChatGPT or Claude):

  • Zero Personal Data Training: We teach every single student the golden rule of AI: Never type your real name, address, or school name into an AI chatbot.
  • Safe Sandboxes: Whenever possible, we use private, secure coding environments so your child's creative ideas and prompts are not absorbed by public tech companies.

4. Bank-Level Security

When you book a visit or enroll your child, your data is locked down. Our website uses secure encryption. Our contact forms are protected by advanced spam-blockers, and any payments you make are handled by certified, world-class payment processors. We never store your raw credit card numbers on our own servers.

5. You Are In Control

You have total control over your data. If you ever want to update your contact details, see what information we have, or ask us to completely delete your records after your child graduates, simply let us know. We will handle it immediately.
